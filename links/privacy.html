Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Ahoy! is a tiny app for sending one-tap "Ahoy!" hails to friends. We keep as little as we can, only to make that work.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and your username. If you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your email (or Apple's private relay address) from them; we don't receive or keep your password for those accounts.
  • Your crew: who you've added, who you've asked to add, and who you've blocked.
  • Ahoy counts: a record of each Ahoy sent (sender, receiver, phrase, time). It powers counts, badges and phrase unlocks. Your total sent is visible to people who can find you.
  • Push tokens: a device token per phone so we can deliver notifications, plus the device name your phone reports.
  • Reports and feedback: if you report someone or send feedback, we keep what you wrote, who it was about, and the app version.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, no analytics SDKs.

Who processes it

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). Notifications are delivered through Expo, Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging, which only see the notification itself. Report emails to the developer may be sent through Resend. They act only on our behalf.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, username, crew, blocks, push tokens, feedback, the reports you filed and the Ahoys you sent. Ahoys you received stay in the sender's count but no longer point to you. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke Ahoy's access.

Can't get into the app? Contact us via the support link on the App Store or Google Play listing and we'll delete it for you.

Children

Ahoy! isn't directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
