Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 (v2)

Ahoy! is a tiny app for sending one-tap "Ahoy!" hails to friends. We keep as little as we can, only to make that work.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, your username and an account ID. If you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your email (or Apple's private relay address) from them; we don't receive or keep your password for those accounts. Google sign-in also sends your name and profile-photo link, which our sign-in provider stores with your account; Ahoy doesn't show them to anyone.
  • Your crew: who you've added, who you've asked to add, and who you've blocked.
  • Ahoy counts: a record of each Ahoy sent (sender, receiver, phrase, time). It powers counts, badges and phrase unlocks. Your total sent is visible to people who can find you.
  • Push tokens: a device token per phone so we can deliver notifications, plus the device name your phone reports. After each notification we keep the delivery receipt ID for up to 24 hours to spot phones that no longer exist, then delete it.
  • Reports and feedback: if you report someone or send feedback, we keep what you wrote, who it was about, and (for feedback) the app version and platform.

Usernames you search for are used to answer the search and not stored. Like any web service, our providers keep short-lived server logs (for example sign-in events with IP address) for security.

All of it is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and linked to your account. It's used only to run Ahoy: your account, your crew, sending and counting Ahoys, notifications, and handling reports and support.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, no analytics SDKs.

Who processes it

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in). Notifications are delivered through Expo, Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging, which only see the notification itself. Report emails to the developer may be sent through Resend. They act only on our behalf.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, username, crew, blocks, push tokens, feedback, the reports you filed and the Ahoys you sent. Ahoys you received stay in the sender's count but no longer point to you. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke Ahoy's access.

Can't get into the app? Follow the steps on the support page and we'll delete it for you. Logging out removes that phone's push token.

Children

Ahoy! isn't directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
