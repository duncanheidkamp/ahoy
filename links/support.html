Support

Lost at sea? Here's how to reach the captain.

Contact

  • In the app (fastest): Settings → Send the crew a message (the message in a bottle). It reaches the developer with your username and app version, so we can help without asking twice.
  • Email: use the in-app message for now; an address will be listed here.

Common fixes

  • No notifications: allow notifications for Ahoy in your phone's Settings, then open Ahoy once. On Android also check that the Ahoys notification category isn't silenced and battery optimisation isn't stopping Ahoy.
  • Can't log in: use the same sign-in you used before (email + password, Apple or Google). Email accounts from the old web app work unchanged.
  • Invite link opens the website, not the app: install the app first, then tap the link again.
  • Someone is bothering you: long-press them in your crew → Block or Report. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account, then type your username. This deletes your account and its data right away; see the privacy policy for exactly what is removed.

Can't get into the app any more? Send a request from the email address on your account (address above) with your username, and we'll delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.
